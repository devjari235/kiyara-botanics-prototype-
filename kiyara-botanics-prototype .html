Setup Requirements

Here's exactly what Kiyara Botanics needs to provide, and what we handle on our end.

📱 WhatsApp Business Number You provide
A dedicated WhatsApp number for Kiyara Botanics (can be an existing business number). We register it with the WhatsApp Business API.

What you need:
  • A phone number (can be a new SIM)
  • Facebook Business Manager account
  • GST/Business registration document
🛍️ Product Catalogue You provide
A simple list of your current products with names, key ingredients, skin concerns they address, and prices. Even a basic spreadsheet works.
🔗 Order/Store Integration We handle
We connect the AI to your existing store (Shopify, WooCommerce, or a custom site) to pull order data for repeat order nudges and abandoned cart recovery. No dev work needed from your side.

From documentation to live deployment:
  • Day 1–2: WhatsApp API setup & approval
  • Day 3–5: AI training on Kiyara's products
  • Day 6–7: Flow testing & refinement
  • Day 8–10: Go live + monitoring

Abandoned Cart Recovery

Trigger: Cart abandoned for 1 hour
System detects the customer left without purchasing and queues a WhatsApp message.

Friendly first reminder (1 hr)

Value nudge (24 hrs — if no purchase)

Final offer (48 hrs — optional)
